# Hanyu (Lotus) Ke

MSc in Quantitative Finance at Singapore Management University, based in Singapore. Interested in market risk and quantitative investment: building models, testing them honestly, and explaining what the numbers mean.

## Projects

- **[multi-asset-var-es-backtest](https://github.com/lotushansstudio-maker/multi-asset-var-es-backtest)**: one-day VaR and Expected Shortfall for a stock-bond-gold-FX portfolio with five models (historical simulation, EWMA, GARCH, GJR-GARCH, filtered historical simulation), backtested 2008 to 2026 with regulatory tests.
- **[sgx-value-screen-backtest](https://github.com/lotushansstudio-maker/sgx-value-screen-backtest)**: a value screen of Singapore-listed stocks and a point-in-time backtest of high-dividend-yield portfolios since 2010.
- **[sp500-trend-filter-backtest](https://github.com/lotushansstudio-maker/sp500-trend-filter-backtest)**: does a 10-month moving-average rule reduce the S&P 500's drawdowns? Tested 1950 to 2026 with robustness checks.
- **[quant-job-search-workspace](https://github.com/lotushansstudio-maker/quant-job-search-workspace)**: a bilingual web app that helps quantitative-finance students in Singapore find a suitable role, the firms that hire for it, and their next step.
- **[Crypto Portfolio Lab](https://lotushansstudio-maker.github.io/AssetPricing-Dashboard/)**: interactive dashboard for an SMU asset-pricing group project on stabilising mean-variance optimisation for a crypto portfolio.

## Daily Visual · 每日视觉

I trained as an oil painter; these days I also paint with code. Small generative and interactive pieces, each written from scratch as a single HTML file. 油画出身，现在也用代码画画。

- **[Light Cage · 光笼](https://lotushansstudio-maker.github.io/visual/light-cage/)**: a particle sculpture in a glowing wireframe cube; hold it in your palm, pull the cage open with both hands and it scatters into a Lorenz-attractor flow, make a fist to reshape it. ([code](https://github.com/lotushansstudio-maker/lotushansstudio-maker.github.io/tree/main/visual/light-cage))

- **[Poem Curtain · 诗帘](https://lotushansstudio-maker.github.io/visual/poem-curtain/)**: classical Chinese poems hang from five kinds of eaves as curtains of characters, each rope a Verlet chain; brush through and they part like cloth, then sway back. ([code](https://github.com/lotushansstudio-maker/lotushansstudio-maker.github.io/tree/main/visual/poem-curtain))

- **[Lumen Tide · 光潮](https://lotushansstudio-maker.github.io/visual/lumen-tide/)**: guide a glowing orb across a night sea of tens of thousands of points of light, driven by a 2D wave equation; tap to splash, hold and release to burst, or wave at the camera. ([code](https://github.com/lotushansstudio-maker/lotushansstudio-maker.github.io/tree/main/visual/lumen-tide))

- **[Dust Sculpture · 本来无一物，何处惹尘埃](https://lotushansstudio-maker.github.io/visual/dust-sculpture/)**: over a hundred thousand points of light form a glowing sculpture that morphs through nine shapes; swipe to blow it into dust, hold for a vortex, release for a shockwave. ([code](https://github.com/lotushansstudio-maker/lotushansstudio-maker.github.io/tree/main/visual/dust-sculpture))

- **[String Mandala · 弦线曼陀罗](https://lotushansstudio-maker.github.io/visual/string-mandala/)**: pin *i* is tied to pin *i × k*; drag *k* and hundreds of glowing strings morph from a cardioid into a flower. Tap to pluck. ([code](https://github.com/lotushansstudio-maker/lotushansstudio-maker.github.io/tree/main/visual/string-mandala))

- **[ASCII Xmas Tree · 手势圣诞树](https://lotushansstudio-maker.github.io/ascii-xmas-tree/)**: thousands of ASCII characters grow into a Christmas tree with one-hand camera gestures, or with taps. ([code](https://github.com/lotushansstudio-maker/ascii-xmas-tree))
