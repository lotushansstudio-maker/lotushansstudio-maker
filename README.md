# Hanyu (Lotus) Ke

MSc in Quantitative Finance at Singapore Management University, based in Singapore. Interested in market risk and quantitative investment: building models, testing them honestly, and explaining what the numbers mean.

## Projects

- **[multi-asset-var-es-backtest](https://github.com/lotushansstudio-maker/multi-asset-var-es-backtest)**: one-day VaR and Expected Shortfall for a stock-bond-gold-FX portfolio with five models (historical simulation, EWMA, GARCH, GJR-GARCH, filtered historical simulation), backtested 2008 to 2026 with regulatory tests.
- **[sgx-value-screen-backtest](https://github.com/lotushansstudio-maker/sgx-value-screen-backtest)**: a value screen of Singapore-listed stocks and a point-in-time backtest of high-dividend-yield portfolios since 2010.
- **[sp500-trend-filter-backtest](https://github.com/lotushansstudio-maker/sp500-trend-filter-backtest)**: does a 10-month moving-average rule reduce the S&P 500's drawdowns? Tested 1950 to 2026 with robustness checks.
- **[quant-job-search-workspace](https://github.com/lotushansstudio-maker/quant-job-search-workspace)**: a bilingual web app that helps quantitative-finance students in Singapore find a suitable role, the firms that hire for it, and their next step.
- **[Crypto Portfolio Lab](https://lotushansstudio-maker.github.io/AssetPricing-Dashboard/)**: interactive dashboard for an SMU asset-pricing group project on stabilising mean-variance optimisation for a crypto portfolio.
